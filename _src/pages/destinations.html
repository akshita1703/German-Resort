Waterfall

Hundru Falls

The Subarnarekha river drops roughly 98 metres over a wall of rock. It's one of Jharkhand's highest falls, at its most dramatic just after the monsoon.

  • {{icon:route}}~45 km
  • {{icon:calendar}}Jul–Feb
  • {{icon:camera}}Day trip
Photo: Kingshuk Mondal, CC BY 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
Waterfall

Dassam Falls

The Kanchi river splits into several streams and tumbles into a deep pool ringed by rocks. A popular picnic spot.

  • {{icon:route}}~40 km
  • {{icon:calendar}}Jul–Feb
  • {{icon:users}}Family picnic
Photo: Subhojit.sil, CC BY-SA 3.0, Wikimedia Commons
Waterfall

Jonha Falls

Also known as Gautamdhara. A long flight of steps leads down through the forest to the base of the falls.

  • {{icon:route}}~40 km
  • {{icon:calendar}}Jul–Feb
  • {{icon:mountain}}Short hike

Scenic drive

Patratu Valley

A ribbon of hairpin bends winds down green hills to the Patratu reservoir. One of the most photographed drives in the state.

  • {{icon:route}}~35 km
  • {{icon:calendar}}Jul–Mar
  • {{icon:camera}}Sunset drive
Photo: Jharkhand Tourism Department, CC0, Wikimedia Commons
Temple

Jagannath Temple

A 17th-century hilltop temple built in the style of Puri's Jagannath. Its Rath Yatra fair draws huge crowds every year.

  • {{icon:route}}~10 km
  • {{icon:calendar}}All year
  • {{icon:diya}}Rath Yatra
Photo: Divya Prakash Bhardwaj, CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikimedia Commons
Temple

Pahari Mandir

A Shiva temple on top of Ranchi Hill, with sweeping views over the city. It's busiest during the month of Shravan.

  • {{icon:route}}~3 km
  • {{icon:calendar}}All year
  • {{icon:mountain}}City views

In the city

Tagore Hill

A rocky hill in Morabadi linked with Jyotirindranath Tagore, the poet's elder brother. A short climb to a quiet pavilion and wide views.

Suggested day plans

Make a day of it.

Ideas for guests with a free day, before or after a wedding, or on a long weekend.

{{icon:waves}}

The waterfall circuit

Hundru, Jonha and Dassam in one long day. Leave early, pack a picnic, and wear good shoes for the steps.

{{icon:temple}}

Temples & heritage

Pahari Mandir at sunrise, Jagannath Temple mid-morning, then the chariot-shaped Sun Temple at Bundu.

{{icon:city}}

Ranchi in half a day

Tagore Hill, Rock Garden and a sunset walk at Kanke Dam. An easy outing for elderly guests.
