Our vision

A place Ranchi didn't have.

For years, families in Jharkhand have travelled out of state for a big wedding or a proper resort holiday. Venues here meant compromises: a banquet without stays, a lawn without parking, a hotel without space to celebrate.

German Resort was planned to end those compromises. We are building private-pool villas inspired by Phuket and Bali, rooms as large as any city hotel's, a 17,500 sq ft pillar-free banquet, a temple with a mandap beside the pool, and a dedicated zone for Mehendi and Haldi. It is one destination where Ranchi can celebrate, stay and unwind, without leaving home.

Design philosophy

Planned around people.

We are aiming to be one of the largest resort destinations in Jharkhand. But what we care about most is how the place feels to the people in it.

{{icon:sparkle}}

Dual-purpose by design

Holiday spaces and wedding spaces are planned together, so a family on vacation and a wedding party of hundreds each get the resort they came for.

{{icon:route}}

Guest-flow planning

Separate back and service passages keep staff movement out of sight, so your guests only see the celebration.

{{icon:shield}}

Privacy first

Villas with their own pools and green buffers. Functions in their own zones. Your gathering stays yours.

{{icon:expand}}

Space without compromise

A pillar-free grand banquet with a very high clear height, plus rooms sized to match, or beat, the city's hotels.

{{icon:car}}

Parking, solved

Car parking at reception and a larger parking area, so the baraat arrives smoothly. Parking is a problem at most venues. Here it is planned in.

{{icon:leaf}}

Lots of green

Lawns, landscaping and open decks give the resort the calm of a tropical retreat, minutes from the city.
